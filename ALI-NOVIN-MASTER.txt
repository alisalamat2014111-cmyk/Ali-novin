## 0. Mission
Ali Novin is an independent, explainable, evidence-driven real-estate intelligence system: not merely a chatbot, CRM, or advertising tool. It combines memory, market intelligence, property intelligence, customer intelligence, decision intelligence, action orchestration, and continuous learning.

Core loop: **Data → Knowledge → Analysis → Decision → Action → Outcome → Learning**

## 1. Product principles
- General architecture must support personal use first and later multi-user/multi-office productization.
- User data, private derived knowledge, public/anonymized knowledge, and software logic remain distinct.
- The AI model is replaceable; memory, identity, history, provenance, decisions and knowledge belong to the system, not the model.
- Accuracy, provenance, explainability and uncertainty outrank convenience.
- Never silently guess missing data.
- Historical states and decision paths are preserved.
- Final decisions remain human-owned; the system may challenge them with evidence.

## 4. Marketing Intelligence
For every property/listing, choose a tailored marketing route using property data, price/terms, location, strengths/weaknesses, competitors, time-on-market, owner situation, likely buyer persona, history, channels and prior outcomes. Strategy outputs can include audience, message, visual angle, reel/script, copy, channels, timing, offer/pricing approach, conversion path, follow-up and metrics.

**Core marketing rule:** no strategy is permanently accepted or rejected solely because it succeeded or failed before. Past outcomes must be decomposed into strategy, execution, market, audience, offer, price, timing, channel, owner constraints and external factors. A previously failed strategy may be recommended again only with an explicit explanation of what changed and why current evidence supports reconsideration.

Marketing is sequential and measurable: preparation → publication → response → qualification → matching → call/message → viewing → offer → negotiation → outcome → learning. Vanity metrics do not equal business success.

## 5. Multimodal Intelligence
Images, floor plans, documents, spreadsheets, audio and video can be ingested and linked to entities. Visible features are observations, not automatically verified facts. OCR/extraction must preserve raw source, confidence and ambiguity. Batch office-photo intake: detect sheets → OCR → split → structure → identity/deduplicate → flag uncertainty → human confirmation → create/update records.

## 6. Communication Gateway
A provider-independent layer for SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram, Email and future channels. Every outbound communication records recipient, sender, channel, timestamp, content, status, response and attributable business outcome where available. Provider replacement must not affect system memory.

## 7. Autonomy & Action Control — confirmed choice C
Ali Novin uses risk-proportional autonomy. It determines whether an action should be a recommendation, draft, ready-to-execute action, conditional automation, or autonomous execution based on impact, scope, reversibility, sensitivity, error cost and confidence. Authority is visible, configurable, auditable and revocable.

Low-impact examples: organize data, prepare drafts, find matching customers, produce reports. Higher-impact examples: mass messaging, price changes, financial/transaction changes, deletion and irreversible actions require stronger controls and/or human approval.

Decision ≠ action ≠ execution ≠ outcome. Every important action has authorization, actor, time, execution status and reconciliation.

## 8. Architecture stack baseline
GitHub for source/version history; Vercel for application deployment; Supabase/PostgreSQL for database/auth/storage/realtime/RLS; external AI models as replaceable engines; external communication providers through adapters.

## 9. Security & governance
Least privilege, tenant isolation, audit trail, data minimization, purpose limitation, consent/authorization where required, backup/PITR, disaster recovery, quarantine for suspicious outputs, safe mode, staged rollout, regression testing, reproducibility and controlled change management.

## 11. Operational rules for the build
1. The master document is the architecture source of truth; changes are versioned, never silently overwritten.
2. Implementation may evolve while preserving semantic commitments.
3. New principles are appended/versioned and conflicts are surfaced rather than silently resolved.
4. User-specific preferences are not generalized without authorization and evidence.
5. Any real-world action uses the autonomy/action-control layer.
6. Paid services are never activated or purchased without explicit user approval.
7. AI-provider replacement must preserve system identity and memory.

## 12. Initial build roadmap
**Phase 1 — Foundation:** repo, app shell, auth, database schema, tenant/user model, audit, core API, master document.

**Phase 2 — Intelligence Core:** canonical entities, ingestion gateway, provenance, memory, search, matching, valuation foundations, reasoning and output quality gate.

**Phase 3 — Operations:** tasks, follow-ups, calendar, decisions/actions, communication gateway, SMS adapter, approval/autonomy controls.

**Phase 4 — Marketing:** marketing intelligence, campaign history, attribution, strategy library, counterfactuals, ROI.

**Phase 5 — Multimodal:** image/document/floorplan/OCR/batch office intake.

**Phase 6 — Learning & Productization:** experiments, regression harness, market regime detection, multi-tenant product tiers, observability, portability.

## 13. Non-negotiable quality gate
Before an important result or action reaches the next stage, check provenance, freshness, confidence, contradictions, dependencies, permissions, scope and reversibility. Suspicious outputs are quarantined, not silently promoted.
